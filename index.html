Peifeng Ji（冀培丰）

Principal Investigator

Peking University First Hospital

Beijing, China

Dr. Peifeng Ji (冀培丰) is a Principal Investigator at Peking University First Hospital. The Ji Lab focuses on developing and applying spatial multi-omics technologies to understand disease mechanisms at molecular resolution.

Our research integrates spatial metabolomics, spatial proteomics, and computational biology to decode the spatial organization of complex tissues. We develop novel strategies at the intersection of mass spectrometry imaging, microfluidics, transfer learning, and single-cell genomics to reveal how cellular heterogeneity contributes to disease progression.

Key research highlights include:

  • High-resolution spatial proteomics based on microfluidics and transfer learning (Cell, 2025)
  • Microbiota-gut-brain axis investigation using single-cell multi-omics (Cell Genomics, 2025)
  • Small extracellular vesicle analysis at single-cell resolution (Nature Methods, 2024)
  • Circular RNA expression landscape and isoform quantification (Cell Reports, 2019; Genome Medicine, 2019)
  • Metagenome assembly and microbial community analysis tools (Nature Communications, 2017, 2022)
