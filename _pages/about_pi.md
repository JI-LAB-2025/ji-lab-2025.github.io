Dr. Peifeng Ji (冀培丰) is a Principal Investigator at Peking University First Hospital. His research focuses on developing and applying spatial multi-omics technologies to understand disease mechanisms at molecular and spatial resolution.

The Ji Lab integrates spatial metabolomics, spatial proteomics, and computational biology to decode the molecular architecture of complex tissues, with applications in metabolic diseases, neurological disorders, and cardiovascular diseases.

#### Research Focus
- Spatial metabolomics and spatial proteomics
- Mass spectrometry imaging and microfluidic workflows
- Computational biology for multi-modal tissue analysis
- Translational disease mechanism studies

#### Positions
- **Principal Investigator**, Peking University First Hospital, Beijing, China
