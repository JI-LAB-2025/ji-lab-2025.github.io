<div class="home-lede">
  <p>
    Dr. Peifeng Ji (冀培丰) is a Principal Investigator at Peking University First Hospital. The Ji Lab develops and applies <strong>spatial multi-omics technologies</strong> to understand disease mechanisms at molecular resolution.
  </p>
  <p>
    We combine spatial metabolomics, spatial proteomics, mass spectrometry imaging, microfluidics, transfer learning, and single-cell genomics to read complex tissues as organized molecular systems.
  </p>
